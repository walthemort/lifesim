Lifesim privacy

Last updated: 17 October 2026.

Lifesim is an offline, single-player life simulator. It stores characters, game progress and backups on your device. It does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, a developer-run game server, or your real location. Choosing a fictional birthplace does not access device location.

The app uses local Preferences for game saves and temporary files for backup exports. Your game data leaves the device only when you choose a destination through the system share sheet. A backup may contain character names, relationship details and choices; review where you send it. Imported JSON files are read to restore your chosen game.

The developer does not collect your game data. Removing the app may remove local progress. Export a backup before uninstalling. Safari and the installed iPhone app have separate storage; moving a save between them requires an explicit export/import.

All money, investments, criminal scenarios, relationships and companies in the game are fictional. No real trades, gambling, payouts or purchases occur. Pro is currently a free local preview, not a billed subscription.

Support

Report issues at the Lifesim issue tracker. Do not post a save file publicly unless you choose to share its contents.

Country and settlement data is bundled from the sources credited in the repository's data directory. It is not a record of the player's actual location.
