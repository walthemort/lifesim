Location database: country metadata from https://github.com/mledoze/countries (ODbL; license alongside); city names from https://github.com/lutangar/cities.json, derived from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0; license alongside). Snapshot downloaded 12 October 2026. src/places.json is the derived, deduplicated city database, distributed under CC BY 4.0; country metadata remains under ODbL; source and attribution must remain with redistributions. Names are real settlements; local schools, employers and sporting venues are fictional. This contains all 250 countries and territories in the source and over 150,000 named settlements, not every hamlet in existence. Country immigration categories are deliberately simplified game rules, not current legal advice.

`src/currencies.json` derives ISO-country-to-currency metadata from the same mledoze country database under ODbL. Exchange rates, country indices and tax rules in `src/economy.js` are fictional simulation parameters, not a live financial or legal data feed.
