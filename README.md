# Lifesim

A single-player life simulation with a Football Manager-inspired dashboard. Start at birth, grow through childhood and school, then build a life through sport, music, content creation, entrepreneurship, investing, or regular employment.

## Run locally

Use Node.js 22.12+ (Node 24 is verified) and npm.

```sh
npm ci
npm run dev
```

Vite uses port 5173 by default. The game runs entirely in the browser: no database, account, API keys, or paid services are required. Fonts are served locally. `npm run build` produces the static site in `dist/`; `npm run preview` serves that build for validation.

## Play

- Choose **Your character → Create a new character** to customize your name, city, personality and initial interest. Every new life starts at age zero, with parents, no personal cash, no completed qualifications and no job. A preferred interest unlocks local participation when you are old enough; it never awards a professional contract. A new life replaces the current browser save.
- **Childhood & school** follows infancy, toddler years, preschool, primary school at five, secondary school at twelve and adulthood at eighteen. Free supervised play and family time develop movement, language, curiosity and confidence. Reading, exploring, friends and homework unlock as you grow. Parents cover basic needs and local club fees before twelve. Allowance begins at six ($5/week), rises to $25 at twelve with $15 personal costs, and ends at eighteen. School graduation at eighteen awards a qualification if grades are sufficient. Existing adult saves retain their ages and money; adult employment still requires age eighteen.
- Choose a **weekly focus** and advance with **Next week**. You earn income, pay living expenses, develop skills, and see market prices change. Demanding weeks drain energy; rest and training support wellbeing.
- **Work & education** has ordinary vacancies with qualification, skill, age, and relevant experience requirements. Apply using a free-time action, wait for a response, then explicitly accept an offer. The new medicine, nursing, teaching, engineering, trades, hospitality and law paths invite qualified applicants to an interview after one week; you have three weeks to attend, and the decision follows a week later. Other applications take two weeks. Eligibility does not guarantee selection. The board refreshes every four weeks; you can apply while employed. Professional playing and driving contracts are handled by the sports pathway, and never appear as job-board applications.
- **Sport & development** starts football through a school team or local amateur club. Choose a position and training plan, play seasonal matches, build ability and coach trust, and potentially receive an academy trial. Trials take two weeks and can fail; an offered academy place still needs acceptance. Academy development can lead to a development-squad contract, then senior club offers. Age, sustained participation, fitness, ability, discipline, and results matter. Contracts last 104 weeks; expiry stops wages, while a proven player can seek another club through scouting. Football injuries require recovery, and retirement ends playing income.
- Motorsport begins with karting, then driver development and offered F4, F3, F2, and F1 seats. National, international, and top-level licences gate progression. Higher categories require relevant professional experience, and tuition and competition costs need funding. Basketball develops from local or school participation through academy and league offers. Tennis develops through local competition, academy, and tour sponsorship; professional tournament wins can earn prize money in addition to sponsorship income. You can keep unpaid hobbies alongside a job; signing a paid sporting contract replaces your existing paid contract.
- Study for certificates, degrees, and racing licences. Tuition is charged weekly. The study focus advances one study week; other focuses advance half a study week. Unaffordable tuition pauses progress. A degree also satisfies a certificate requirement in the same field. A qualification unlocks eligible applications only after graduation; withdrawing forfeits partial progress and paid tuition.
- Employment contracts have their own pay, tenure, and sustained performance. Discuss a raise after 13 weeks, then every 26 weeks; it can be refused. Strong performance can earn a 3% annual merit increase. Sporting pay changes through offered contracts. Well-connected characters can occasionally receive unusual job introductions with different entry requirements.
- **Age up** simulates real weeks to your next birthday and pauses for a pending decision or the end of your life. It never automatically selects a choice or marks your save as debug. Young children receive annual childhood choices; lives begun at birth receive quarterly teenage choices. Adult and legacy teenage lives keep their two-week choices. **Next week** remains available for detailed play. Resolve pending choices before continuing.
- **Weekly calendar** divides 168 hours between nightly sleep, work, school, commuting, personal care, parenting and optional pursuits. Automatic planning follows active paths; custom planning allocates 0–40 hours per pursuit. Sixteen allocated study hours complete one course week. Overbooking spreads the remaining time across optional activities, slows training, delays creative projects and harms wellbeing and work performance. Paid sports include training in working hours. Ordinary actions also consume hours, while next-week dates and family days reserve time. The displayed forecast includes actions already taken. Low sleep has health and energy consequences.
- Three free-time actions are available each week, subject to the time budget. **Relationships** gives people personalities, interests, priorities, trust, mood, earnings and their own plans. Contacts can begin work, receive annual wage growth, or relocate. Meet offline, or use a phone/computer to browse an adult dating app every thirteen weeks. Replies and invitations can be refused. Dating starts at sixteen with peers; adult dating cannot target a minor. Plan a park walk, coffee, dinner, concert, gallery visit or video call for the next week. The venue and conversation affect the outcome, and busy or exhausted people can miss a booking. Cancelling before the date refunds 80% of reservations.
- Three good dates, 65 connection and 65 trust let you discuss becoming exclusive; they can still decline. Engagement requires age 18+, 52 weeks together, 80 connection and 75 trust. Marriage is a separate decision on **Family & pets**, at least four weeks later. People have their own preferences about children.
- Workplace mentoring develops relevant skills; meetings build manager and colleague connections. Internal promotions require the next role’s qualifications, experience and 70 performance, followed by a review and explicit acceptance. A new role has a thirteen-week probation review. Sustained performance below 45 produces warnings; three formal warnings can end employment. Eight hours of overtime cost calendar time; nursing, trades and hospitality pay time-and-a-quarter for the added hours, while salaried roles do not pay extra. Optional pension contributions of 3–15% receive an employer match up to 2%; savings count toward wealth and can be withdrawn from sixty.
- **Investments** offers fictional listed-company shares, an index fund, crypto, bonds, and a property fund. Crypto supports fractional units; other assets use whole units. Trades have a $1 minimum and weekly liquidity limits. Horizon Technologies and Solstice Energy shares represent actual ownership percentages; controlling more than 50% grants management control. Funds distribute quarterly income, and companies pay dividends when profits and cash allow it. A negative personal balance carries 0.5% weekly interest.
- Collect watches, sports memorabilia, art, and classic cars when dealer listings appear. Estimated values fluctuate. Reselling requires a two-week auction; a high reserve can leave an item unsold. Successful auctions deduct an 8% commission.
- **Lifestyle & shopping** has 61 possessions: everyday essentials, clothing, music and creator equipment, training gear, hobbies, vehicles, homes, and luxury travel. Filter by category, search, sort by price, or show only owned items. Some purchases have age restrictions and recurring upkeep. Equipment supports particular habits or production quality. An owned home replaces rent, while every owned property's maintenance remains payable. Ordinary possessions sell for 80% of their purchase price; buying a luxury item does not buy an audience.
- Eight repeatable experiences include dinners, concerts, travel, football camps, creative workshops, and networking. Each uses cash and a free-time action, has a cooldown, and changes wellbeing, connections, craft, or training.
- **Social media & fame** offers Lens photos, Loop short videos, ViewTube long videos, and Streamroom live streams. Accounts require age 13+ and a phone or computer; publishing needs appropriate recording equipment. Choose a niche, privacy, and authentic, polished, or clickbait content. Each post costs cash, an action, and energy; at most two posts can be produced per week. Audience results arrive the following week. Content skill, equipment, subject knowledge, reputation, and luck affect reach. Inactivity loses followers, and viral posts are rare.
- Recent public accounts with 1,000 followers can earn advertising income. Brands can approach accounts with 5,000 followers and strong reputation. Accepting a collaboration earns no immediate cash: deliver labelled public content with sufficient quality within three weeks. Missed delivery earns no fee and harms reputation. Private accounts retain their existing audience but do not earn advertising or sponsorship income through discovery. Fame also reflects sporting reputation and music fans.
- **Music & creativity** supports singers, rappers, and producers. Choose a role and genre, practise vocals, lyrics, and production, then record after eight sessions. A release costs $150, requires recording equipment, and takes four weeks. Streams generate simulated royalties of $0.002 each, while audiences and production quality affect results. Local gig invitations depend on craft and practice; booking earns a fee only after a successful performance. Established artists can receive label offers: a 52-week agreement pays an advance, retains half the royalties, and recoups the advance from your share before further royalty cash is paid.
- Found a company with 30 business skill and $5,000 of startup capital. Name it, choose an industry, and decide how much positive profit to draw or retain. Revenue, costs, company cash, staffing, and valuation develop over time. You can keep a job while running it as a side business; quarterly distributions then depend on actual profits and cash.
- **Businesses & clubs** includes two football clubs, a GT racing team, an F1 team, private companies, and listed companies. Rare sale windows offer particular stakes for six weeks. Otherwise, pay a nonrefundable introduction fee, wait two weeks, and hope the owners agree to hear an offer. They may refuse the meeting or reject a low bid. Approaches have a 26-week cooldown.
- Submit a funded bid for a private business or a large block of listed shares. It reserves your cash while owners decide; acceptance still requires explicit closing within three weeks. Withdrawing, rejection, or expiry releases the reservation. A majority stake lets you set that business's strategy; private businesses also accept capital injections. Minority stakes earn proportional dividends. Sports results use a seasonal schedule, with weekly football outings and alternating-week racing outings during the active season.
- **Homes & property** adds three rental arrangements and six property types. Renting transfers a refundable four-week deposit and costs $100 to move. Purchase prices change every 13 weeks; you can own up to six homes. Cash purchases pay 2% closing fees. A mortgage has a 20% down payment, 2% fees, fixed 6% annual interest, and weekly payments over twenty years. Approval requires 26 weeks in employment, twelve payments of reserves after closing, and property commitments within 35% of wages. Moving into a purchased home returns your rental deposit.
- Investment properties have upkeep even when vacant. Advertising costs $150 and tenant reviews begin after four weeks. A signed one-year lease earns rent after a 10% management fee; you can give notice to prevent renewal at expiry. Occupied homes cannot be immediately sold or reclaimed. Sale reviews start after four weeks, can fail, and deduct 5% before repaying the mortgage. Missed mortgage payments accrue interest; thirteen consecutive misses trigger repossession at 85% of estimated value. The property market and underwriting are fictional simplified models.
- **Family & pets** supports engagements, city-hall weddings ($300), celebrations ($3,000), family planning, adoption, and separation. Starting a family needs age 21+, a year together, 80 connection, 70 trust, a partner who wants children, and at least two bedrooms. Birth preparation costs $1,000 and takes 39 weeks; the simplified adoption route costs $3,000 and takes 26 weeks. Children grow up on the weekly clock, start school at five, and need attention. Support costs $45/week under five, $65 at ages 5–12, and $90 for teenagers; employed parents also pay $45 childcare for under-fives. Basic support ends at eighteen. Children develop interests, confidence and independence; school activities from age five and private education add costs and care time. Football, karting (from six), music, coding and art clubs cost $10–$45/week; private school adds $90/week. Quarterly school concerns can be met with listening, tutoring or pressure, each with different consequences. At eighteen, education and interests influence their own university, creative or apprenticeship choice. You can commit to $60/week support for university until twenty-one. Book a five-hour family day for $25 to strengthen relationships. Moving in together needs an adult partnership, trust and your own home; your partner contributes from their own wages and savings, and parenting hours are shared. Parenting focuses trade energy for connection or education. Separation preserves existing child responsibilities.
- Adopt a dog ($200, $14/week care) or cat ($125, $10/week care) from age 18 with a private home. Care uses an action and improves connection; vet checks cost $80 every 26 weeks. The simplified companion lifespan is thirteen years for dogs and seventeen for cats; departed pets remain in your household memories.
- **Hobbies & journal** adds writing, photography, coding, cooking, gardening, and art/design. Practice uses an action, 6 energy, and a small materials budget; some crafts need a computer, phone, or camera. Ten sessions and 25 skill allow a project, which costs $60–$180 and takes 4–12 weeks. Completed projects form a portfolio and can earn uncertain small sales for a year. Adults with 40 skill and a completed piece can occasionally receive paid commissions; delivery costs materials and energy and can fail. Community volunteering improves the likelihood of introductions. Daily routines offer frugal living, healthy habits, or meal preparation unlocked by 30 cooking skill.
- Twelve persistent life milestones record things such as your first pay cheque, homeownership, a creative release, parenting, or reaching $1 million. Twelve additional contextual life choices cover parenting, neighbours, pets, creative opportunities, and public recognition.
- A life ends at age 90 or when health reaches zero. You can review your story or create a new life.

## New in v0.9

- **All four sports gain deeper professional careers.** Footballers and basketball players can seek club/team transfers, higher-tier contracts and twenty-six-week development loans. Drivers negotiate racing seats; tennis players change training bases and tour sponsors. These are opportunities earned after the existing school, academy and recruitment pathways, rather than athlete vacancies. Qualifications, sporting skill, ability and experience remain mandatory for moves and are checked again when signing.
- Market windows differ: football uses weeks 0–7 and 26–33, basketball 0–7 and 35–45, racing 0–3 and 39–51, while tennis bases/sponsors can change year-round. Requests take at least two weeks and wait for an open window. Injuries, reputation, agent quality and release permission affect the outcome. Requests have a thirteen-week cooldown; the current contract continues until a live offer is explicitly signed. Permanent moves support the existing one-time counteroffer system. Loans retain the original parent contract and pay, then return after twenty-six weeks without erasing development; quitting does not reinstate employment later. Racing continues to require competition licences and a thirteen-week cash reserve for uncovered driver funding.
- **Work & education** and **Sport & development** expose the new activities for every sport. Team, engineering and coaching discussions improve chemistry and trust. Role reviews can improve playing time or technical support, with uncertain decisions; starter promises are reviewed against actual appearances. Hire a personal coach/race engineer ($350 upfront, $70/week) and physiotherapist ($200 upfront, $45/week). Coaches support development and physiotherapists shorten recovery. Staff keep billing until dismissed, including after retirement.
- New destinations have career bases. Keeping your household in another city adds three committed travel hours each week and can strain relationships. Relocating to a different city costs $1,500, requires adulthood and a partner's agreement, temporarily stops their wages while they seek work, and can unsettle children. A loan returns your sporting contract; it does not automatically move the household back. National squad invitations and racing/tennis exhibitions can arrive for established athletes. Booking reserves eight hours next week; club participation is unavailable that week, and the appearance fee requires successful delivery.
- **Music & creativity** and the career screen add artist collaborations and producer commissions. Musicians aged fourteen or older with twelve practice sessions, a release and listeners may receive time-limited opportunities. Managers improve access and negotiation chances. Shared projects pay an artist fee plus the normal $150 recording budget, record the agreed master ownership share and develop through the existing recording process. Your share is applied before label splits, recoupment, manager commission and tax. Producer client work pays once after on-time delivery at the required quality; the client owns streaming royalties. Counteroffers take two weeks, with no acceptance while terms are pending.
- Musicians aged sixteen or older can sequence three to eight owned released tracks into an album for $250 and four hours. Mastering takes two weeks and promotes the existing catalogue, preserving ownership shares without creating duplicate royalty streams. Each track can belong to one album. Live rehearsal develops readiness once per week, including DJ/production sets for producers.
- **Touring has real budgets and risk.** Adults need two owned releases, sufficient music fans, setup cash and a buffer for two show budgets. Local tours have three shows, require 100 fans and eight hours per show week, and cost $120 setup plus $80/show. Regional tours have five shows, need 1,000 fans and twenty-four hours, and cost $1,000 setup plus $1,000/show. International tours have six shows, need 10,000 fans and forty hours, and cost $7,500 setup plus $9,000/show. Add a live team for $200/show or full production for $1,000/show. Longer tours require leaving paid employment or sport first; local shows can coexist with a job. Shows start two weeks after planning. Craft, rehearsal, crew and energy influence performance; ticket demand is limited by actual fans and venue capacity. Venue, crew and travel costs remain payable when a show fails. The tour ledger records tickets, gross receipts and production costs; management commission and tax further reduce take-home profit. Cancellation costs 25% of remaining show commitments, retaining setup and completed-show expenses.
- Authentic v0.8 saves retain all existing fields, including relatives, school results, ageing, managers, studios, catalogue, contracts, banking and family. New moves, loans, staff and touring begin without invented past agreements. Existing releases retain full artist ownership before their original label terms. The browser preserves an exact downloadable pre-v0.9 save.

## New in v0.8

- **Living families:** choose a modest, comfortable or wealthy household when creating a life. Your parents have their own earnings and savings; choosing wealth does not put cash into your newborn’s wallet. A younger sibling arrives during childhood, starts school, goes to university, graduates into engineering and can move away for a promotion. Relatives retire at sixty-seven onto 55% of their former weekly pay. Their health declines in later life, and medical care can help. Visits build connections; sending $100 transfers actual money, while arranging care costs $50.
- Students aged sixteen to twenty-five can request education support every thirteen weeks from living parents with at least 65 connection and 60 trust. Grants draw from 2% of available family savings, capped at $100/$500/$2,000 for modest/comfortable/wealthy households. Existing households use the $500 cap. Parents can refuse because of finances or relationships; this never grants a degree.
- Relatives can die in later life, with their memories and contacts preserved. A deceased parent’s actual savings are divided between the other surviving parent and children. The surviving parent receives half when present; the remainder is shared between you and surviving siblings. Your share is exempt income, or held in a trust until eighteen. Death cannot repeatedly pay an estate. NPC relatives’ savings are separate from your net worth until transferred.
- **School life:** choose balanced, academic, creative or sporting priorities. Academic focus requires three additional weekly hours. Debate, science, arts and volunteering clubs develop relevant skills and require two or three weekly hours; science and arts cost $5/week, paid by parents before twelve and by you afterwards. Classmates can become real friends or rivals, with contextual school choices and lasting contacts.
- Secondary-school exams happen in week 49 of each year, from twelve through seventeen. Results combine current grades, knowledge and revision preparation. Preparing costs an action, three hours and energy, adds ten preparation points (maximum fifty), and preparation resets after an exam. Results influence school grades and are saved in your exam record.
- School-age part-time jobs are available at sixteen and seventeen: shop assistant (eight hours, $72/week), café helper (twelve hours, $108/week), and peer tutor (six hours, $84/week, requiring 80 school grade). Their hours enter the calendar and exhausted students can miss wages. Earnings are taxable and recorded work hours count toward office, hospitality or teaching experience at forty hours per full-time-equivalent week. They end at eighteen; adult jobs require a separate application.
- A merit scholarship requires school graduation, age eighteen to twenty-five and an exam average of 85 across at least two recorded exams including the final year. It pays half of tuition for 156 paid enrolment weeks, does not stack with an existing tuition grant, and never awards qualifications. Planning university, an apprenticeship, work or a creative route only records your intention; actual courses and vacancies retain their requirements.
- **Health & ageing:** fitness declines gradually after thirty-five, mobility after forty-five and health after sixty. Athletic ability decline varies by age and sport, with racing peaking later. Fitness and lasting injury wear affect performance and injury risk; older athletes can take longer to recover. Conditioning, mobility work and paid rehabilitation take actions and hours. Rehabilitation costs $80 and reduces injury wear without instantly healing an active injury. Fitness and mobility can also be edited through debug controls.
- Experienced footballers can retrain through a twenty-six-week coaching certificate costing $50/week, requiring school graduation, age eighteen, 30 athletics and 52 professional football weeks. Academy coach vacancies then use the normal qualified application and offer system. The health screen also links to retraining and media options after sport.
- Authentic v0.7 saves preserve cash, family, banking, sporting seasons, music managers, equipment and progress. The new systems begin without invented past exam results, injuries or family memories. New family backgrounds do not replace the finances of existing relatives, and an exact pre-v0.8 backup remains downloadable.

## Saves

Progress automatically saves in this browser's local storage. **Save & settings** can export a JSON backup or import one on another device. Imports are validated and require confirmation before replacing the current life. If a stored save cannot be read, its original contents are preserved as an exportable recovery copy. Browser data deletion clears local progress, so export backups regularly. The game has no cloud sync.

Version 0.1 through 0.8 saves upgrade automatically, preserving contract pay, cash, investments, founded companies, and owned clubs. Existing professional players and drivers keep their contracts; outstanding athlete job applications are retired in favour of development and scouting. The browser keeps an exportable **pre-update save** before automatic conversion. Its storage key stays unchanged so existing lives are found. Existing version 0.4 households, mortgages, children, pets, projects and debug records stay intact. Version 0.4 and earlier saves gain automatic calendar planning, empty pensions, and profiles for existing contacts and children. Version 0.5 and 0.6 calendars, pensions and developed households remain intact. Version 0.6 banking, tax histories, sporting seasons and ongoing commitments also remain intact; new career support starts without a manager or campaign. Version 0.7 managers, studios and childhood records remain intact; new family, school and ageing records begin at migration rather than inventing a past. New bank accounts start with zero savings and no personal loans; taxes begin at migration, with current market prices establishing the cost basis of existing holdings. No previous earnings are taxed retrospectively. For saves older than 0.4, housing, family and debug systems initialize with no loans, children or debug changes; existing partners remain and their partnership-duration clock starts at migration.

Net worth includes cash, investments, founded-company valuation, private ownership stakes, collectible estimates after commission, possessions at their resale value, refundable rental deposits, and property values minus mortgage debt, locked pension savings, and bank savings minus personal-loan debt. Listed-company ownership is counted once through its shares. Company valuations and collectible estimates are paper wealth. Submitted and accepted bids reduce uncommitted cash, which is used to determine whether new purchases are affordable.

## Debug sandbox

Open **Save & settings → Enable debug mode**. The game keeps a pre-debug snapshot that you can export or restore from settings. Restoring replaces your current life; export it first if you want to keep both. A new character starts with debug off.

Debug controls can add $10,000, $1 million or $1 billion; set exact cash, wellbeing, actions, recognition, career/hobby skills or followers; and grant a known qualification. Reserved acquisition bids still constrain spendable cash. Debug does not award experience or a professional sports contract simply by increasing a skill.

Time skips simulate 1, 4, 13 or 52 actual weeks, including wages, expenses, loans, recruitment, market movements, creative results and deadlines. Pending life choices use their last option; the final week's choice remains available. A skip stops if the life ends. Normal choices and purchases still have prerequisites.

Debug changes persist across reloads and exports. A visible sandbox label and the last twenty debug edits stay with the save even after disabling controls. Returning to the pre-debug snapshot restores the original regular life.

## Structure and scope

- `src/engine.js`: pure state transitions, deterministic seeded randomness, catalogues, and save validation. Actions return new state rather than modifying the current save.
- `src/world.js`: employment, qualifications, business ownership, negotiations, company operations, and collectible auctions.
- `src/app.js`: dashboard, screen navigation, dialogs, local persistence, and save import/export.
- `src/screens.js`: work, education, business ownership, and collectible interfaces.
- `src/life.js`: school, football development, social platforms, fame, and experiences.
- `src/sport.js`: karting/motorsport, basketball, and tennis development and recruitment.
- `src/music.js`: creative craft, recording, royalties, performances, and label agreements.

This is a playable foundation with fictional employers, markets, platforms, courses, and licences. Recruitment, audience growth, creative earnings, and ownership have prerequisites, waiting periods, and uncertain outcomes. Matches, races, tournaments, music distribution, algorithms, staffing, and company operations use simplified models. Cities share one economy. Actual sports regulations, copyright/licensing administration, jurisdiction-specific tax and full banking/credit rules, legal tenancy and adoption procedures, insolvency, and private-business exits are not yet modeled.

Possible expansions include deeper squad management and transfers, business recruitment and product launches, private-business exits, relocation and distinct economies, deeper family histories, employee recruitment, product launches, and a larger event/item catalogue. Keep these systems connected to the existing life, time, and financial simulation.
